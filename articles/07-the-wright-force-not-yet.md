# The Wright Force, Not Yet: A Field Theory of Monumental Self-Regard
*A specimen of emergentology conducted under the eye of a hostile zoological review board*

**Classification:** mathematically explicit toy model and speculative EFMW-inspired proposal. **No fifth fundamental physical force is established.**

### Abstract
The editor-in-chief hereby nominates himself for the chair of a discipline he has just invented, then submits the nomination to peer review by a tortoise and a very skeptical crocodile. Both animals request units.

### A field that is an update law rather than a proclamation
Let \(\phi(x,t)\) be a scalar state on a bounded domain \(\Omega\subset\mathbb R^d\). With diffusion coefficient \(D>0\), relaxation rate \(\kappa>0\), and target evidence field \(s(x,t)\), consider
\[
\partial_t\phi=D\Delta\phi-\kappa(\phi-s).
\]
For time-independent \(s\) and homogeneous Neumann boundary conditions, stationary states solve
\[
-D\Delta\phi_*+\kappa\phi_*=\kappa s.
\]
Define an energy
\[
\mathcal E[\phi]=\int_\Omega\left(\frac D2|\nabla\phi|^2+\frac\kappa2(\phi-s)^2\right)dx.
\]
Formally, for sufficiently smooth solutions,
\[
\frac{d\mathcal E}{dt}
=-\int_\Omega|\partial_t\phi|^2 dx\le0.
\]
Thus this **specified gradient-flow model** dissipates its chosen energy. It has not thereby explained consciousness, cosmology, elections, or the editor's overdue electricity bill.

### Units, or the crocodile bites
If \(\phi\) and \(s\) share units \([\Phi]\), then \([D]=L^2/T\), \([\kappa]=1/T\), and \([\mathcal E]=[\Phi]^2L^d/T\) with this unnormalized functional. A physical energy requires a constitutive scale with matching dimensions.

### The EFMW comparison, explicitly limited
The user-proposed EFMW scalar equation
\[
\nabla^2\phi-c^{-2}\partial_t^2\phi=
(4\pi/c^2)(E+Pc)
\]
is wave-like, whereas the toy model above is parabolic and dissipative. A mapping requires definitions and units for \(E,P,\phi\), a well-posed limiting procedure, conditions, and comparison with measured phenomena. Calling both "fields" does not make them equivalent.
