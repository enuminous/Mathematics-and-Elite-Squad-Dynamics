## ACT III: THE TRUMP IMPEACHMENT PARADOX

Hegseth has compared the effort against him to the proceedings against Trump. There is a small logical difficulty in treating Trump's history as evidence that impeachment itself cannot occur.

Trump was actually impeached twice. The Senate acquitted him both times.

In other words, impeachment happened; conviction did not.

For the purposes of our satire, we have submitted this reasoning to the **EFMW Institute for Extremely Flexible Meanings of Words**.

**Experimental proposition:** An event that happened did not happen because its subsequent consequence failed to happen.

- House impeachment: YES.
- Senate conviction: NO.
- Satirical institutional conclusion: THEREFORE THE FIRST BOX NEVER EXISTED.
- Logical validation: **UNRESOLVED**.

We tested this proposition against ordinary mathematics.

Suppose I receive two parking tickets.

Suppose neither results in my car being impounded.

By the mock interpretation, I have never received a parking ticket.

Suppose I then receive an eviction notice.

By the same reasoning, the building has never existed.

Follow the logic far enough and the United States has never had a Secretary of Defense.

*This last proposition has been referred to the Department of Philosophy for independent replication.*

## ACT VI: THE FOUR HORSEMEN OF THE NON-ANSWER

The fictional EFMW rhetorical laboratory now isolates four experimental techniques:

1. **The Patriotism Substitution:** Replace a question about an official decision with a declaration of love for the country.
2. **The Persecution Loop:** Treat a request for evidence as proof that the questioner is persecuting you.
3. **The Trump Precedent Generator:** Reference an unrelated political survival story until procedural distinctions collapse.
4. **The Infinite Non-Answer:** Repeat "I will fight" without identifying which legal argument is being advanced.

These are satirical caricatures of argumentative maneuvers, **not a finding about any particular future testimony**.

The interesting mathematical characteristic is that none of these operations requires an answer to the original question.

A hearing may therefore continue indefinitely while the amount of information supplied remains approximately zero.

In the EFMW parody model:

{\operatorname{RhetoricalVolume}(t)}

## FINALE: THE CONSTITUTION HAS ENTERED THE CHAT

There is, buried beneath the theatrical smoke, a serious institutional question.

Congress has the authority to demand accountability from executive officials. Those officials may contest allegations and defend their records. The impeachment process exists precisely because a constitutional republic cannot rely exclusively on officials to supervise themselves.

Hegseth is entitled to dispute the allegations and make his case. Congress is entitled to examine the evidence. And the public is entitled to distinguish a persuasive answer from an impressively loud one.
