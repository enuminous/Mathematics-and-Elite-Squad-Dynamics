# Can It Run Without Jensen?

*Dispatch 24 · Satirical opinion · November 24, 2026*  
By Matthew Chenoweth Wright

The most offensive word in Jensen Huang’s offer to return to Nvidia was *America*. He had already mentioned the shareholders, the employees and the company. A lesser man, applying for his old job five days after his resignation became public, might have considered that a sufficient number of references. Jensen put down the United States of America. Imagine having to call the other candidates. [Huang’s November 17 statement](https://abcnews.com/Business/nvidia-forms-national-security-committee-feds-probe-company/story?id=138206932)

I’ll give him this. His apology to his family contained a sentence of admirable precision: “I was unfaithful to my wife.” Subject, verb, injured party. Nobody had to convene a committee to establish what he meant. By the time he was discussing his return to work, his ability to do the job had become a matter for the republic. Somewhere in that journey from *my wife* to *America*, a man who owed people an explanation acquired a country that apparently owed him a favor. [The apology](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/a-message-to-our-company) · [The offer to return](https://abcnews.com/Business/nvidia-forms-national-security-committee-feds-probe-company/story?id=138206932)

The charge here is vanity on a scale that ordinarily requires a building permit. The espionage allegation concerning Alice Huang remains unresolved. She has already done enough unpaid work as the woman whose photograph illustrates every American man’s decision in this story. [What the intelligence reporting establishes](https://abcnews.com/amp/US/woman-center-nvidia-scandal-touch-chinas-top-spy/story?id=138369034)

Anyway, Jensen had a perfect right to ask for his job back. This is where my prosecution of Jensen Huang runs into a small procedural difficulty. **Somebody had to give it to him.**

On November 17, Harvey Jones told investors that the board had unanimously accepted Huang’s resignation and had no intention of reinstating him. On November 23, Nvidia announced that the board had unanimously reinstated him. Its investigation would continue. I find the second *unanimously* almost touching. Whatever changed in that room, the directors managed to preserve one small comfort. Nobody had to go home having disagreed in public. [November 17 transcript](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/content_files/NVDA-3Q27-Earnings-Transcript.pdf) · [November 23 announcement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm)

Directors can change their minds. Occasionally one hires them in the hope that they have minds available for that purpose. Nvidia says the directors spoke with Huang, satisfied themselves about his candor and heard from senior U.S. officials about the national-security implications of his return. Donald Trump duly claimed credit for intervening. Fine. Which answer did Huang give that persuaded a director who believed on Tuesday that the company needed a successor? I would read ten pages about that conversation. Instead I have a press release telling me how confident everybody was at the end of it. [The board’s explanation](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm) · [Trump’s account to CNBC](https://www.reuters.com/technology/trump-tells-cnbc-he-was-represented-nvidia-board-spoke-huang-2026-11-23/)

Perhaps we should bring in the gamers. They spent years asking whether an expensive piece of hardware could run *Crysis*. A generation of young men who wanted more convincing sunlight on an imaginary puddle helped make a graphics-chip salesman famous. I feel some affection for these idiots. At least they knew how to test the salesman’s claim. You could turn on the frame counter. If the puddle looked magnificent at eleven frames per second, the gentleman in the leather jacket was going to hear about it. [The history of the Crysis benchmark joke](https://www.pcgamer.com/crysis-remastereds-highest-graphics-setting-is-called-can-it-run-crysis/)

The AI crowd has developed a more expensive relationship with the gentleman. Nvidia reported $107.6 billion in data-center revenue in a $115.8 billion quarter. The category includes more than AI, but it does suggest we can let the kid with the puddle go home. Consider the executive who has promised his own board an AI transformation, committed the budget and announced the partnership. Ask him for an independent opinion of the man on whose product roadmap his next three years depend. Give him a moment. He has to find an opinion that also preserves his job. [Nvidia’s quarterly results](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)

And here I sit, commissioning an AI to write a scathing attack on the people who keep finding uses for AI. I could have worked the irony out with a pencil. This was faster. If we’re taking attendance, mark me present.

So is it all of us? I understand the appeal. There’s something almost generous about passing the blame around until a teenager who bought a used graphics card owes the same apology as a director who voted twice. By dinner everybody has confessed and nobody has to resign.

Huang owns what he did and what he told his directors. Trump owns the pressure he chose to apply. Those of us who buy the products, cheer the keynotes or ask the machine for one more draft can own the appetite that makes a founder feel indispensable. The directors had a different job. Their job was to exercise judgment precisely when the founder, the customers, the market and the president had all made their preferences abundantly clear. Anyone can tell you what the room wants. You pay a board for the possibility that it might say no.

Nvidia announced that Huang was back. The stock went up more than 31 percent. I can see how a director might look at the closing price and feel that events had settled the question. But try the same reasoning in reverse. If reinstating him had knocked 31 percent off the stock, which fact about his candor would have changed by four o’clock? [Associated Press on the vote and the market close](https://apnews.com/article/nvidia-jensen-huang-39d4b34ffaa867b4e669893b5fd5028f)

Put my name down for the essay. Put the gamer down for the puddle. Put Jensen down for listing America as a reference. And if the directors would still like me to accept my share of responsibility for reinstating him, they can send me the minutes.

I’d like to see how I voted.
