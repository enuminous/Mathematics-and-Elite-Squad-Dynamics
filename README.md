### Editorial standard
Every article separates **proven statements inside explicit toy models**, **testable hypotheses**, and **satire**. A rigorous proof of a toy model does not verify its political metaphor. EFMW and the Zoo are subjects of investigation, not axioms exempt from scrutiny.

### Submission policy
We favor brief, auditable manuscripts; specify variables, units, boundary conditions, counterexamples, baselines, and failures. A provocative title is a privilege, not a substitute for evidence. Do not fabricate quotations or citations. The journal is a satirical publication, not a peer-reviewed scientific periodical.
